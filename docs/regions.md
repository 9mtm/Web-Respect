# Regional guidance v2 — reviewed 2026-10-10

The maintained [regional research profiles](../skill/web-respect/references/regional-sources.md) ship with the agent skill. They cover initial EU/EEA, Brazil, United States and Australia research, with official discovery sources for additional markets.

Start with [owner intake and applicability](../skill/web-respect/references/applicability.md). Operator establishment, markets actually targeted/served, processing, entity size, sector and audience affect which rules apply. Website language or hosting country alone cannot settle applicability.

Every audit must verify current primary sources, rules, amendments, effective dates and actual configuration. Installation, badges, or a successful automated check do not certify legal or WCAG compliance.

Use the [bundled skill](../skill/web-respect/SKILL.md), [data-flow inventory](../skill/web-respect/references/data-flows.md), [privacy operations](../skill/web-respect/references/privacy-operations.md), [accessibility evaluation](../skill/web-respect/references/accessibility-audit.md) and [report requirements](../skill/web-respect/references/audit-report.md).
