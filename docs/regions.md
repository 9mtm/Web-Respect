# Regional guidance v1 — reviewed 2026-10-09

Engineering guidance; not legal advice or a compliance certificate. Determine host/service applicability and recheck primary sources before production. Profiles gate all optional purposes off by default but are not interchangeable descriptions of the law.

## EU/EEA

Where consent is the legal basis, use a free, specific, informed and unambiguous choice with practical withdrawal. Do not assume consent is the only lawful basis for all processing. Explain purposes/services independently and assess national terminal-equipment rules. Source: [EDPB lawful processing guide](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/process-personal-data-lawfully_en).

Provide visible refusal and granular choices without deceptive presentation or preselected optional categories; examine actual notice/button behavior, not merely the saved JSON. Source: [EDPB cookie banner taskforce report](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/system/files/2023-01/edpb_20230118_report_cookie_banner_taskforce_en.pdf).

Open host work: policy text, applicability/exemptions, provider contracts and transfers, retention rationale, server-side tracking, requests and child/guardian rules. Applicable consent ages and safeguards depend on jurisdiction/service; a browser checkbox cannot verify them.

## Australia

Assess whether Privacy Act/APP obligations apply, what pixels disclose, sensitivity of information and actual collection/notice/consent duties. Sensitive information and overseas disclosures need particular review; an EU cookie banner is not a universal statement of Australian requirements. Source: [OAIC tracking pixels guidance](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-guidance-for-organisations-and-government-agencies/organisations/tracking-pixels-and-privacy-obligations).

Open host work: APP applicability, collection notices, purposes/necessity, sensitive-information consent, overseas disclosures, provider configuration and privacy-request operations. Resolve age/guardian and consumer issues for the actual service with qualified advice.

## Accessibility

The host must build and audit accessible content, keyboard interaction, contrast, text resize, focus and assistive-technology support. Toolkit controls cannot certify the host or repair inaccessible canvas/3D experiences. Source: [W3C WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/).

## Extending profiles

Add a dated country/region document with primary sources, applicability assumptions, required operational work and explicit unsupported issues. Keep choices and inventory configured by the host. Never add a generic all-country compliance badge or treat installation as legal review.
