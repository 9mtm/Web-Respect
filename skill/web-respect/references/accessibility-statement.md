# Accessibility statement workflow

Preparing or updating an accessibility statement is a required Web Respect website-integration step. Inspect the host's existing statement first and preserve its verified facts and routes. For audit-only work, report findings and a draft instead of publishing. Ask for missing owner/contact information, covered URLs/services, review dates, evaluation method, known barriers, accessible alternatives and applicable national complaint/enforcement procedures. Reuse facts already established by the audit.

Determine the actual legal scope before describing this page as legally mandatory. Directive (EU) 2016/2102 Article 7 requires statements for covered public-sector websites/apps; Implementing Decision (EU) 2018/1523 provides the model. Directive (EU) 2019/882 Article 13 and Annex V require accessibility information for covered services, subject to scope, exemptions and national implementation. Do not claim every private European website has the same statement obligation. Verify current national legislation and the appropriate enforcement body for the owner's markets.

Generate an accessible host-styled page from actual evaluation evidence and the template in `templates/accessibility-statement.md`. Choose compliance wording only when supported by an appropriate assessment against the applicable standard; an unknown result remains unassessed, never automatically fully compliant. Include known non-accessible content with reasons and useful alternatives, preparation/review dates and evaluation method, contact/feedback details, and verified complaint/escalation routes where applicable. For covered EAA services, include the service description, operation and how relevant accessibility requirements are met. Do not invent authorities, response deadlines, audit dates or fixes.

Replace all placeholders before publication. Keep uncertain facts in the private review notes; do not expose internal audit records. If required facts are missing, report the statement step as pending. Preserve the host language and design, semantic headings, keyboard access and useful link labels. Set `policies.accessibility` to the actual published route; the accessibility drawer footer shows Feed first and Statement beside it. The statement page alone has one small text-only `Powered by Dpro` link to `https://dpro.at/`, without a Dpro logo. Do not add this credit to the accessibility drawer itself.

Verify statement facts against the audit, configured route, contact links, applicable requirements, mobile/keyboard rendering and owner-reviewed publication scope. Review and refresh the statement when coverage, known barriers or the evaluation changes. Publication is not certification.

Primary sources: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2016/2102/oj, https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec_impl/2018/1523/oj, https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/882/oj. Verify national implementation and current regulator guidance before applying them.
