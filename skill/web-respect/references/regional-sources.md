# Regional research starting points v2

Reviewed 2026-10-10. Engineering research guidance, not legal certification. Recheck sources and current applicability on every audit. National/state implementation, sector rules, amendments and effective dates can change. These profiles do not exhaust each jurisdiction's law.

## EU/EEA

Assess establishment and offering/monitoring scope using the [EDPB territorial-scope guidelines](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/documents/guideline/guidelines-32018-on-the-territorial-scope-of-the-gdpr-article-3-version-adopted_en). Determine purposes and processing bases using the [EDPB lawful-processing guide](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/process-personal-data-lawfully_en). Separate GDPR analysis from national terminal-equipment rules. Examine refusal, granular choices and withdrawal against the [EDPB cookie taskforce report](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/system/files/2023-01/edpb_20230118_report_cookie_banner_taskforce_en.pdf).

Research rights, storage limitation, processor obligations and transfer safeguards in the current GDPR text and regulator sources. Do not assume every dataset relies on consent, every transfer is prohibited, or EU hosting alone resolves transfers. Investigate national age rules and sector-specific obligations.

For accessibility, assess the service and national implementation of the [European Accessibility Act](https://commission.europa.eu/strategy-and-policy/policies/justice-and-fundamental-rights/disability/european-accessibility-act-eaa_en), including scope/exemptions and relevant dates. Public-sector obligations need their own source review. Technical WCAG testing and legal applicability are distinct.

## Brazil

Read current [LGPD legislation](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2018/lei/l13709.htm), especially territorial scope, processing bases, children, end-of-processing retention and rights. Do not limit assessment to the operator's headquarters. The [ANPD rights FAQ](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/perguntas-frequentes) is a practical entry point; verify rights and exceptions against law.

Use the [ANPD cookies guide](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/materiais-educativos-e-publicacoes/processo-guia-orientativo-cookies-e-protecao-de-dados-pessoais.pdf) for purpose/transparency/choice analysis and distinguish guidance from binding law. For transfers, examine [ANPD Resolution 19/2024](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/institucional/atos-normativos/regulamentacoes_anpd/resolucao-cd-anpd-no-19-de-23-de-agosto-de-2024) and current amendments/mechanisms, rather than copying EU clauses as if interchangeable.

Accessibility scope needs separate review of [Brazilian Inclusion Law, Article 63](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2015/lei/l13146.htm) and the actual operator/service. Verify current implementation and applicable technical requirements.

## United States

Ask for actual customer states and relevant entity/volume/sector facts. Assess each applicable state separately; there is no single cookie-banner configuration that proves nationwide compliance. Use current statutes/regulations rather than a static threshold copied from a general FAQ.

The [California Attorney General CCPA guide](https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa) identifies privacy rights, sale/sharing opt-outs and GPC. Confirm current thresholds, exemptions and regulations; inspect actual downstream behavior. Other states can have different targeted-advertising, sensitive-data, appeal and opt-out rules. The [Colorado official rules](https://coag.gov/app/uploads/2023/03/FINAL-CLEAN-2023.03.15-Official-CPA-Rules.pdf) are a starting point for that state's signal analysis; recheck current amendments and regulator-recognized mechanisms.

If children are in scope, verify [FTC COPPA rule amendments](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/federal-register-notices/16-cfr-part-312-coppa-final-rule-amendments), effective/compliance dates and applicable scope. Health, finance, education and employment require separate federal/state research.

For accessibility, use [DOJ web accessibility guidance](https://www.ada.gov/resources/web-guidance/) and the actual public/private context. Distinguish public-entity rules from private businesses, and check current rule dates and applicable standards. Do not apply one public-sector deadline to all websites.

## Australia

Determine Privacy Act/APP applicability and exemptions for the actual entity/service. Assess pixels and disclosures using [OAIC tracking guidance](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-guidance-for-organisations-and-government-agencies/organisations/tracking-pixels-and-privacy-obligations). An EU banner does not resolve Australian collection/notice/consent duties.

Review overseas recipient arrangements and whether a flow is use or disclosure under [APP 8 guidance](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-8-app-8-cross-border-disclosure-of-personal-information). Inspect protection and destruction/de-identification duties, exceptions and backups under [APP 11 guidance](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-11-app-11-security-of-personal-information). Do not describe Australia as providing an identical general GDPR erasure right. Verify applicable access/correction and sector obligations separately.

Use the Australian Human Rights Commission's [2025 digital accessibility guidance](https://humanrights.gov.au/?a=68431) to inform evaluation under the actual disability-discrimination/service context. Verify current requirements rather than presenting guidance as certification.

## Additional markets

When relevant, start UK analysis at the [ICO storage/access technologies guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/what-are-storage-and-access-technologies/) and research current PECR/UK GDPR changes. Start Canadian analysis at the [OPC consent guidance](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/p_principle/principles/p_consent/) and investigate federal/provincial scope. These are discovery links, not complete additional profiles.

For other markets, find official legislation/regulator materials and build a dated applicability profile. Never infer worldwide coverage from this list.

## Technical accessibility baseline

Use [W3C WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) for criterion-level technical evaluation when appropriate. Record the version/level required by applicable law or contract. Inspect underlying content and journeys with manual assistive-technology testing as well as automated checks. Preferences cannot repair inaccessible source content or establish conformance on their own.
